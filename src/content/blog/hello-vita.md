This is a native post: it lives in the repo, renders on its own route, and carries every optional field at once. The `cover` shows in the ledger and on the post page, `series` groups related posts, `readingTime` renders as a mono readout, and tags feed the blog's filter pills.

Story links from other content types (an interest, a book, a speaking entry, a city) can point at a post like this one, so a one-line record connects to real writing. Half of Wren's demo record ends up here, which is exactly the point.

## Markdown works everywhere

Bodies are Markdown: **bold**, `code`, lists, and headings all render through the shared prose styles.
